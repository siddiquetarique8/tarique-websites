# Tarique's Websites

A simple directory of Tarique's websites and projects.

## T.S. Welds

[Visit T.S. Welds](https://tsweldtech.site.je/)

T.S. Welds is a welding and metal fabrication business site serving Kilokri, New Delhi. It introduces the business and its custom fabrication services, including gates, railings, rolling shutters, industrial sheds, metal stairs, structural work, and general welding. Visitors can review the services and contact the business to discuss a project or request a quote.
